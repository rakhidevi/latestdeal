A **Lightning Deal** is a time-bound promotional offer on Amazon where a limited number of discounts are offered on an item for a short period. 

## Key Characteristics

1. **Time Limit:** Lightning Deals typically last between 4 to 12 hours, but they can end sooner if the promotional inventory is fully claimed.
2. **Quantity Limit:** The deal is only valid for a specific number of units. Once 100% of the units are claimed, the deal ends, even if there is time left on the clock.
3. **One Per Customer:** You are generally limited to purchasing one unit of a product at the Lightning Deal price.

## The Waitlist

If a Lightning Deal reaches 100% claimed, you might see a "Join Waitlist" button. If a buyer adds the deal to their cart but does not complete the purchase within 15 minutes, the item is released to the next person on the waitlist.

## Pro Tip for Prime Members

Amazon Prime members often get **Prime Early Access** to Lightning Deals, allowing them to view and claim deals 30 minutes before the general public. During major sales events, this early access is crucial for securing high-demand items.
